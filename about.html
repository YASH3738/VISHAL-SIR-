DR. VISHAL YOGI

Senior Clinical Physiotherapist focused on meaningful recovery.

Dr. Vishal Yogi is a Senior Clinical Physiotherapist (BPT, MPT-Sports, MBA-HA, PGDY, CNMT) with more than 10 years of experience and over 10,000 patients treated.

He is currently working with Shalby Hospital Jaipur, Tagore Hospital Jaipur and Dr. Dheeraj Dubey's orthopaedic and joint replacement team. His previous experience includes Manipal Hospital Jaipur.

He specialises in orthopaedic and neurological rehabilitation, with additional expertise in cupping and sports taping. His qualifications include a BPT from Rajasthan University of Health Sciences, MPT in Sports Physiotherapy, MBA-HA from Government University, Kota, a PG Diploma in Therapeutic Yoga from Government Sanskrit University, Jaipur, and CNMT certification.

He is a Life Member of the Global Association of Physiotherapy and has received the Best Clinical Physiotherapist Award 2025 and Young Physio Achievement Award 2024, among other professional recognitions.
