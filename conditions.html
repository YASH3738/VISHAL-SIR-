Movement & Function

Your symptoms are individual.

Pain or reduced movement can affect the way you work, walk, exercise and manage everyday tasks.

Physiotherapy assessment can help understand movement and functional limitations and guide an appropriate rehabilitation approach.

Every condition is different. Treatment and rehabilitation should be based on an individual assessment rather than a generic programme.

Before Your Appointment

Bring your story.

A useful consultation starts with understanding what you are experiencing.

If available, bring relevant medical reports, prescriptions, imaging reports and details of previous treatment or rehabilitation.

Tell the physiotherapist about your symptoms, movement difficulties and the activities that are affected.

Medical reports

Bring relevant reports or prescriptions if available.

Previous treatment

Share relevant previous treatment or rehabilitation information.

Movement concerns

Note which movements or activities are difficult for you.

Your questions

Keep any questions you would like to discuss during the consultation.
