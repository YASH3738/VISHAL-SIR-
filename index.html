ABOUT DR. VISHAL YOGI

About Dr. Vishal Yogi

Dr. Vishal Yogi is a Senior Clinical Physiotherapist (BPT, MPT-Sports, MBA-HA, PGDY, CNMT) with 10+ years of experience and 10,000+ patients treated. He specializes in orthopaedic and neurological rehabilitation.

He is currently associated with Shalby Hospital Jaipur, Tagore Hospital Jaipur, and Dr. Dheeraj Dubey's Orthopaedic & Joint Replacement Team. He previously worked with Manipal Hospital Jaipur.

With expertise in physiotherapy, rehabilitation, pain management, and therapeutic yoga, Dr. Yogi focuses on personalized, evidence-based care to help patients recover, move better, and improve their quality of life.
